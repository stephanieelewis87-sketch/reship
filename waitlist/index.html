What's inside

Everything a reseller juggles, finally under one roof.

Here's a peek at what Hubby is built to do. We're polishing each part before we open the doors.

SellSync: list once, sell everywhere

Crosslist to the big resale marketplaces from one place, keep prices and details in sync, and stop double-selling the same piece.

Inventory that knows where things are

Every item, its SKU, what you paid and the bin it lives in. Turn a pile of finds into listings faster with help from AI.

Shipping labels in one print

Sold on five different apps today? Print all of those labels together, with SKUs and packing slips, and track what's out the door.

Returns without the headache

Keep each return in one list, know what's coming back and why, and get the item back on the shelf and relisted.

Your true profit, not a guess

Sales, fees, shipping and what you paid, added up for you. See what's selling, what's stuck and where your best finds come from.

The Hangout, and meetups

A friendly community to swap tips, ask questions and celebrate sales, plus local meetups to source and learn with sellers near you.

Our mission

To make it possible for any ordinary person to start and grow a resale business.

You shouldn't need ten apps, a spreadsheet and a business degree to sell what's in your closet. Hubby takes on the busywork so you can spend your time finding, listing and selling.

FAQ

Good questions.

When does Hubby launch?

Soon! We're inviting sellers in waves as each part is ready. Join the waitlist and you'll hear from us the moment your spot opens.

Why is it called Hubby?

It's the hub for your whole reselling business, and the hubby that does it all: it prints, it tracks, it counts and it never forgets where you put that jacket.

Which marketplaces will it work with?

The big resale apps, including Poshmark, Mercari, eBay, Depop and more. Tell us where you sell when you join, and it helps us decide what comes first.

Is it for beginners or full-time sellers?

Both. Hubby is built so someone selling their first ten items feels at home, and it grows with you as your shop does.

How much will it cost?

Joining the waitlist is free. We'll share pricing before you're invited, so there are no surprises.

What will you do with my email?

Only tell you about Hubby and your invite. We'll never sell it, and you can ask us to remove it any time.
